2. TIMELINE OF EVENTS

- 14:08 UTC: Customer support received reports of intermittent HTTP 504 Gateway Timeouts during RAG query ingestion.
- 14:15 UTC: SRE team identified connection pool exhaustion in the ChromaDB persistent client wrapper.
- 14:26 UTC: Hotfix deployment initiated to recycle idle database worker threads and expand max_connections from 20 to 100.
- 14:41 UTC: Error rates returned to baseline (<0.02%); connection queues cleared.
- 14:49 UTC: Incident officially downgraded to closed status after 8 consecutive minutes of healthy health checks.

3. ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS (RCA)
A concurrent batch ingestion job uploaded 45 audio recordings simultaneously, triggering multiple Faster-Whisper 
transcription workers that held open persistent database locks while awaiting disk sync. This starved concurrent 
read queries, driving request timeout cascades across downstream microservices.

4. PREVENTATIVE ACTIONS & REMEDIATION
- Action 1: Enforce asynchronous queueing (Celery / Redis Queue) for audio ingestion jobs.
- Action 2: Decouple read and write connection pools in the persistent client.
- Action 3: Decrease connection keep-alive timeout from 60 seconds to 15 seconds.
